About the zone line

The 10 EMA you place in the Stochastic window is an EMA of price, scaled to whatever price range is on screen, so its reading against 20/80 changes when you zoom. Kora measures it the same way every time: where the 10 EMA sits in its range over the last zone window candles (200 M15 candles is about the 2 days a phone chart shows). The Boom/Crash (yours) chart template on the Charts page draws exactly this line. With settings 1,1,1 the Stochastic lines themselves just jump between 0 and 100 each candle, which is why the EMA is the line that matters.

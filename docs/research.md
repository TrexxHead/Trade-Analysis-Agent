# What works on Kora's instruments: research notes (2026-11-28)

Kora's rule: an idea is only used live once it is **at least 65% likely to be a real edge**. That means it has to:
- survive Kora's own out-of-sample test on your instruments, after costs, and
- be consistent with reliable outside evidence.

Blog posts, YouTube strategies and vendor claims count as *hypotheses*, never as proof.

## Non-negotiables (applied to every strategy, including yours)

| # | Rule | Why | Source |
|---|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Pick settings on the first 60% of history, then judge only on the untouched last 40% | In-sample results are always flattering | Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu, *The Probability of Backtest Overfitting* (J. Computational Finance) |
| 2 | Charge the spread on every trade; extra slippage when a Boom/Crash spike runs the stop | Most "edges" on short timeframes are smaller than costs | standard practice |
| 3 | At least 20 in-sample and 25 out-of-sample trades | Small samples are noise | Minimum Backtest Length (same authors) |
| 4 | Adjust for the number of strategies tried (Šidák on a bootstrap p-value) | The best of many backtests is biased upward | Bailey & López de Prado, *The Deflated Sharpe Ratio* (J. Portfolio Management, 2014) |
| 5 | Test families (all trend followers, all mean-reversion…) as one equal-weight portfolio, with every member included | Stops cherry-picking the winners | Park & Irwin on data snooping in technical analysis |
| 6 | Promote at ≥ 65% confidence and profit factor > 1; re-test weekly; drop on failure | Edges decay; regimes change | — |

The engine is in `src/analyst/research.py`. A test (`tests/test_rules.py::test_research_rejects_noise`) checks that on a pure random walk **nothing** gets promoted.

## Findings (Deriv data to 2026-11-27: M15 ~7 months, H1 ~8–12 months)

### XAUUSD (gold)
- **Outside evidence:**
  - Time-series momentum is profitable across commodity futures, gold included, on a *monthly* horizon (Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012).
  - Intraday moving-average rules have no predictive power at standard settings; there is some power with longer parameters in gold (Batten et al. 2018).
  - Gold's biggest hourly ranges cluster around the US open and the London morning. That is volatility, not direction.
- **Kora's test:**
  - Most trend and breakout systems were positive on unseen data: EMA trend cross +0.24R/trade (81 trades), Supertrend +0.21R, Donchian +0.20R, squeeze +0.18R, Ichimoku +0.34R.
  - Two trend systems were negative (ADX pullback −0.48R, MACD −0.04R).
  - With all members counted, the trend family scored only ~22% confidence. **Not promoted yet.**
  - Mean-reversion and session-breakout systems failed.
- **Verdict:** lean towards trend and breakout methods on gold (H1 rather than M15), but no specific system has earned live use yet. Re-tested weekly.

### XAGUSD (silver)
- **Outside evidence:**
  - Intraday MA (trend) rules show no predictability in silver (Batten et al. 2018).
  - The gold/silver ratio tends to mean-revert over years (Silver Institute, industry-funded).
- **Kora's test:** the **mean-reversion family passed at 88% confidence** (+0.12R per active day out of sample). Its members:
  - Bollinger fade (+0.12R/trade, 130 trades)
  - RSI(2) pullback (+0.04R, 324 trades)
  - Stochastic dip (+0.09R, 55 trades)
- **Verdict:** promoted. Short-term mean reversion on silver feeds Kora's plans. This agrees with the outside evidence that silver lacks intraday trend persistence.

### V75, Boom 1000, Crash 1000 (synthetic)
- **Outside evidence:**
  - These indices are generated by Deriv's algorithm. V75's volatility is fixed at 75%.
  - Boom/Crash 1000 spike on average every 1,000 ticks.
  - There is no economic mechanism for a lasting edge.
- **Kora's test:**
  - No family passed on any synthetic.
  - Several systems looked strong in-sample (e.g. MACD on V75, +0.52R) and then lost on unseen data (−0.47R). That is the classic overfitting signature.
- **Your EMA cross + Stoch zones strategy:**
  - Slightly negative in-sample on both Boom and Crash (−0.01R and −0.14R per trade at a 1R target, spread included), so it never reached the out-of-sample stage.
  - The drift-only variant was positive on Boom M5 sells (+0.46R over 19 trades). That is far too few trades to trust.
- **Verdict:** treat any synthetic-index system as unproven. Keep risk small, stick to the plan, and expect variance. Kora keeps testing.
